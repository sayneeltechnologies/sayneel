Frequently asked questions

Before you book

How long is the consultation?

Thirty minutes, sometimes a little longer if there is a lot to cover. It is a working conversation with an engineer, not a sales pitch.

Is it free?

Yes. The consultation itself is free and comes with no obligation to proceed.

Can I request a BalSaathi demo?

Yes — select "BalSaathi Demo" in the services field and we will walk you through the platform live on the call.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, we will sign your NDA before any detailed discussion, or provide ours if you would rather use one.

How soon can we start?

Most engagements can kick off within one to two weeks of the proposal being approved, sooner if the scope is already well defined.
