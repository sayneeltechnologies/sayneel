Before you write

What to include, and what happens next

📝

Helpful to include

What you are trying to achieve, who will use it, any deadline that matters, and a rough budget range if you have one. Two paragraphs is plenty — we will ask the rest.

⏱️

What happens next

We reply within one business day and propose a thirty-minute call with an engineer. If it looks like a fit, a written proposal follows covering scope, approach, timeline and cost.

🔒

Confidentiality

We will sign your NDA before any detailed discussion, or provide ours. Nothing you send us is shared outside the people evaluating it.

🤷

If we are not the right fit

We will say so on the first call and, where we can, point you towards someone better suited. A clear no is more useful than a slow maybe.

Ready when you are.

One email is enough to start. You will get an engineer's honest read on feasibility, timeline and cost.
